CS-Audit

An open toolkit and benchmark for error-level evaluation of speech recognition on Kazakh and Kazakh–Russian code-switched speech

Problem and gap

  • Voice products need to understand real Kazakh: spontaneous, with Russian words mixed in
  • Whisper-large: 43.6% word error rate on read Kazakh (FLEURS)
  • Open data exist (KSC 332 h, KSC2 > 1,000 h with code-switching)
  • But accuracy is reported as one number on read or broadcast speech
  • Gap: no error-level comparison of read, spontaneous and code-switched speech; no open tool to do it
  • R&D type: applied research + experimental development · TRL 3 now → TRL 5 target

Aim and SMART objectives

O1 · month 8

Test sets ≥ 2 h per speech type + detector F1 ≥ 0.85 on 600 verified real utterances

O2 · month 12

Whisper small / medium / large-v3, a Kazakh model and a commercial API: WER, CER with 95% CI and error classes (κ ≥ 0.70)

O3 · month 17

Fine-tuning with 10, 50, 100 h (3 seeds, 18 runs): is the gain 50→100 h < half of 0→50 h?

O4 · month 18

Open benchmark, toolkit and guide; one paper; workshop with 2 local companies
